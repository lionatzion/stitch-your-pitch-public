# Release notes

## Grok template 0.4.2-r3, platform version 14

Published November 6, 2026 at the [existing public Bot link](https://x.ai/bot/Yaqu1CNc1OG_Ffi-vVWsY).

- Added writing choices for tone, length, punctuation and excluded phrases.
- Set "thrilled" and "passionate" as default cover-letter exclusions, with add, remove, replace, one-request empty-list and reset options.
- Added checks for reporting frequency, who performed an action and the difference between overall experience and employer tenure.
- Kept seven skills in the public template. Setup, tailoring and document review received the writing changes.

All seven saved skill bodies and descriptions matched the reviewed release before and after publication. A bounded fictional owner conversation and local wording checks passed. These checks do not establish independent-user acceptance or general writing reliability.

The Grok details screen still displayed October 29 after this publication. The published content was verified separately; the reason for the stale date remains unresolved. Existing recipient copies have not been verified as automatically updated.

## Beta guide and optional progress reporting

Available November 6, 2026 at [stitchyourpitch.com/beta](https://stitchyourpitch.com/beta/).

- Separate paths for testing drafts and testing the optional scoring connection.

Progress reports are self-reported activity. They do not prove successful authentication, scoring or application submission.
