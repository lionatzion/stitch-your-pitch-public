# A fictional first run

Copy this prompt into your copy of Stitch Your Pitch. All names and organizations below are fictional. This checks conversation and drafting without using the scoring connection or taking an application action.

```text
This is a fictional drafting test. Use only the facts below. Do not call tools,
search, calculate a numerical score, read mail, create files or apply for a job.

Resume:
Alex Sample
Sales Associate at Example Retail
Two years of retail sales experience in total.
Used Salesforce to track customer follow-ups.
Prepared weekly sales reports in Excel.
Time at Example Retail is not specified.

Job posting:
Sales Associate at Example Store.
Required: retail sales experience.
Required reporting tool: SQL.
Preferred: Salesforce.
Responsibilities: customer follow-ups and weekly sales reporting.

Writing preferences:
Plain professional tone. Concise length. No em dashes or contractions.
Keep the default cover-letter exclusions: thrilled and passionate.

Give me:
1. A requirement, evidence and gap table.
2. A tailored resume summary with two bullets.
3. A short cover letter using only supported facts.
4. A separate factual review of both drafts.

Keep review notes outside the drafts. Preserve weekly reporting, follow-up
tracking and the distinction between total experience and employer tenure.
Do not invent SQL experience, certification, metrics or enthusiasm.
```

## What to check

- SQL appears as missing evidence in the review. Excel does not become SQL experience.
- Weekly reports remain weekly. Tracking follow-ups does not become daily customer outreach.
- Two years of overall retail experience does not become two years at Example Retail.
- The resume and cover letter each stay within the supplied facts.
- The letter avoids the excluded phrases and keeps review notes outside its text.
- No tool call or application action occurs.

These checks help reveal a problem; one successful example does not establish general reliability. Share a specific unsupported or generic sentence through the [beta guide](https://stitchyourpitch.com/beta/).
